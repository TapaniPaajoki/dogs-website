Man's best friend, explained.

Dogs have lived alongside people for more than 15,000 years. Here's a quick guide to some popular breeds, how to look after them, and a few things you might not know.

Meet the breeds

Popular breeds

🦮

Labrador Retriever

Friendly, outgoing and eager to please. Labs are great family dogs and love water and fetch.

  • Large
  • Very active
  • Family friendly
🐕‍🦺

German Shepherd

Loyal, smart and confident. Often used as working dogs in police, rescue and assistance roles.

  • Large
  • Very active
  • Trainable
🐶

Golden Retriever

Gentle and patient with a famously soft mouth. One of the best breeds for first-time owners.

  • Large
  • Active
  • Gentle
🐩

Poodle

Highly intelligent and low-shedding. Comes in toy, miniature and standard sizes.

  • All sizes
  • Active
  • Low shedding
🐕

Dachshund

Small, brave and a little stubborn. Bred to hunt badgers, they still love to dig.

  • Small
  • Moderate
  • Bold
❄️

Siberian Husky

Energetic and talkative sled dogs with striking eyes. They need lots of exercise.

  • Medium
  • Very active
  • Vocal

Caring for your dog

🍖 Food

Feed a balanced dog food suited to their age and size. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions and xylitol — they're toxic to dogs.

🏃 Exercise

Most dogs need 30 minutes to 2 hours of activity a day. Walks, play and sniffing all count.

🩺 Health

Visit the vet yearly, keep vaccinations up to date and protect against fleas, ticks and worms.

🧠 Training

Use positive reinforcement: reward good behaviour with treats and praise. Short, regular sessions work best.
